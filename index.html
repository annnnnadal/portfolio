How I work

How I take a project from the first conversation to the final design.

  1. Every project starts with listening: what the project needs, and what the people behind it need too. Experience helps me ask the right questions from day one, and show where design can help long before anything is drawn.

  2. Before shaping anything, I get to know the brand, look at what already exists and search for the value proposition. When a project is already underway, a blueprint workshop helps us see where we stand. I share what I’m finding before we choose a path, often with references or a quick low-fi sketch. A simple visual sparks a better conversation than a concept explained in words.

  3. We set clear milestones, with clear deliverables at each one. Delivering in phases gives strategy the weight it deserves, and gives the visual work a solid reason to exist.

  4. Every proposal is a draft, open to improvement until we reach a shared goal. I bring the team and the client into each decision, because a decision made together rarely gets overturned. Once we agree, that phase is closed and we move forward without going back.

  5. Only now does the final design take shape, built on everything that came before.

  6. Talk early, talk often. With the team and with the client, at every step.

    Even a draft should be beautiful. A rough idea presented with care earns the trust to develop the rest calmly.

    Build to last. When everyone understands why each phase exists, what we create can grow and scale over time.
